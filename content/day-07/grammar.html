🔄 Gerund vs Infinitive

+ Gerund (V-ing)+ Infinitive (to V)
enjoy, mind, stop, suggest, avoid, imagine, finish, keep, miss, practise, consider, risk, deny, postpone, admitdecide, want, hope, promise, agree, plan, offer, refuse, seem, learn, need, expect, would like, ask, help

أفعال يتغير معناها:

  • stop + gerund: يتوقف عن (He stopped smoking.)
  • stop + to: يتوقف ليفعل (He stopped to smoke.)
  • remember + gerund: يتذكر فعل شيء (I remember locking.)
  • remember + to: يتذكر أن يفعل (Remember to lock.)
  • try + gerund: يجرب (Try restarting.)
  • try + to: يبذل جهداً (I tried to lift.)

🔥 Advanced Grammar

🔹 Would Rather / Prefer / Had Better

  • would rather + V1: I'd rather stay home.
  • would rather + فاعل + V2: I'd rather you didn't go.
  • prefer + V-ing to V-ing: I prefer walking to driving.
  • had better + V1: You'd better hurry.

🔸 Possessive 's (الملكية)

  • Ali's car (مفرد)
  • parents' house (جمع منتظم)
  • children's toys (جمع غير منتظم)
  • Ali and Sara's house (ملكية مشتركة)

🔹 Tricky Grammar Points

Used to / Be used to / Get used to

  • I used to smoke. (عادة ماضية)
  • I am used to waking early. (معتاد)
  • You will get used to driving. (ستعتاد)

Another / Other / Others

  • another cup (واحد إضافي)
  • other problems (صفة للجمع)
  • the other shoe (الآخر من اثنين)
  • some voted yes; others voted no.

Much / Many / Few / Little

  • much time (غير معدود – سؤال/نفي)
  • many books (معدود – سؤال/نفي)
  • a few ideas (قليل إيجابي معدود)
  • a little hope (قليل إيجابي غير معدود)
  • few people (قليل جداً سلبي)
  • little time (قليل جداً سلبي)

So / Such

  • He is so tall that...
  • It was such a good movie that...

Although / Despite / In spite of

  • Although he was sick, he came.
  • Despite being sick, he came.

Causative (have/get something done)

  • I had my car repaired.
  • She got her hair cut.

Wish / If only

  • I wish I had more time. (حاضر)
  • I wish I had studied. (ماضي)
  • I wish it would stop raining. (مستقبل)

Embedded Questions

  • Where is the bank? → Do you know where the bank is?
