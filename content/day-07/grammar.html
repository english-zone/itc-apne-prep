🏛️ Passive Voice (المبني للمجهول)

نستخدمه للتركيز على الفعل أو المفعول، أو عندما يكون الفاعل غير معروف.

الزمنActivePassive
Present SimpleThey make cars.Cars are made.
Past SimpleShe wrote a letter.A letter was written.
Future SimpleHe will fix it.It will be fixed.
Present ContinuousThey are building a house.A house is being built.
Past ContinuousHe was painting the wall.The wall was being painted.
Present PerfectShe has cleaned the room.The room has been cleaned.
ModalsYou must do it.It must be done.

🗣️ Reported Speech (الكلام المنقول)

تغيّرات شائعة: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, here → there, this → that.

🔄 Gerund vs Infinitive

+ Gerund (V-ing)+ Infinitive (to V)+ Either (no/slight difference)
enjoy, mind, stop, suggest, avoid, imagine, finish, keep, miss, practise, consider, risk, deny, postpone, admit decide, want, hope, promise, agree, plan, offer, refuse, seem, learn, need, expect, would like, ask, help begin, start, continue, like, love, hate, prefer

أفعال تتغيّر معناها:

  • stop + gerund: يتوقف عن فعل (He stopped smoking.)
  • stop + to مصدر: يتوقف ليفعل شيئاً (He stopped to smoke.)
  • remember + gerund: يتذكر فعل شيء ماضٍ (I remember locking the door.)
  • remember + to مصدر: يتذكر أن عليه فعله (Remember to lock the door.)
  • try + gerund: يجرب طريقة جديدة (Try restarting the computer.)
  • try + to مصدر: يبذل جهداً (I tried to lift the box.)

🏷️ Question Tags (الأسئلة الذيلية)

مثبت + منفي؟ / منفي + مثبت؟

  • You are tired, aren't you?
  • He won't come, will he?
  • She has finished, hasn't she?
  • I am late, aren't I? (استثناء)
  • Let's go, shall we? / Open the door, will you?

🔥 Advanced Expressions

Would Rather / Prefer / Had Better

would rather + V1I'd rather stay home.
would rather + فاعل + V2I'd rather you didn't go.
prefer + V-ing to V-ingI prefer walking to driving.
had better + V1 (تحذير/نصيحة قوية)You'd better hurry.

Causative (have/get something done)

  • I had my car repaired. (أصلحت سيارتي عند الميكانيكي)
  • She got her hair cut. (قصت شعرها عند الكوافير)

Wish / If only (التمني)

حاضر (مستحيل)wish + Past SimpleI wish I had more time.
ماضي (ندم)wish + Past PerfectI wish I had studied harder.
مستقبل (شكوى/أمل)wish + would + V1I wish it would stop raining.

Embedded Questions (الأسئلة المضمنة)

بعد عبارات مثل: Do you know...? / Can you tell me...? / I wonder...

  • Where is the bank? → Do you know where the bank is?
  • What time does it start? → Can you tell me what time it starts?

ترتيب الكلمات: فاعل + فعل (وليس سؤالاً).
