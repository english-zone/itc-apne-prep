🏛️ Passive Voice (المبني للمجهول)

الاستخدام: عندما نريد التركيز على الفعل أو المفعول به بدلاً من الفاعل، أو عندما يكون الفاعل غير مهم أو غير معروف.

🔁 كيف نحول جملة من Active إلى Passive؟

  1. ننقل المفعول به (Object) إلى بداية الجملة.
  2. نضع الفعل be في نفس زمن الجملة الأصلية + past participle (التصريف الثالث).
  3. نضع الفاعل الأصلي (إن أردنا) بعد by.

مثال توضيحي:

Active: The cat 🐱 chased the mouse 🐭.
Passive: The mouse 🐭 was chased by the cat 🐱.
⬅️ المفعول به يتحرك إلى الأمام

📊 جدول الأزمنة في المبني للمجهول

الزمنActivePassivebe + p.p.
Present SimpleThey make cars.Cars are made.am/is/are + p.p.
Past SimpleMy father wrote this book.This book was written by my father.was/were + p.p.
Future SimpleI will do the exercise.The exercise will be done by me.will be + p.p.
Present ContinuousThey are building a house.A house is being built.am/is/are being + p.p.
Past ContinuousHe was painting the wall.The wall was being painted.was/were being + p.p.
Present PerfectShe has written the report.The report has been written.have/has been + p.p.
Past PerfectThey had finished the work.The work had been finished.had been + p.p.
Future PerfectWe will have completed it.It will have been completed.will have been + p.p.

🔧 Modal Verbs في المبني للمجهول

التركيب: modal + be + past participle

  • You must finish the report. → The report must be finished.
  • We can solve the problem. → The problem can be solved.
  • They should respect the rules. → The rules should be respected.
  • Someone may steal the money. → The money may be stolen.
  • We have to clean the room. → The room has to be cleaned.

📌 متى نستخدم المبني للمجهول؟

  • عندما يكون الفاعل غير معروف: My wallet was stolen.
  • عندما يكون الفاعل غير مهم: Coffee is grown in Brazil.
  • في الكتابات العلمية والرسمية: The experiment was conducted in a lab.
