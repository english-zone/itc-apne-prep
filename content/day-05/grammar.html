📊 Comparative & Superlative

🔹 Comparative (المقارنة بين اثنين)

الصيغة: صفة قصيرة + er + than / صفة طويلة → more + صفة + than

  • My sister is younger than me.
  • English is easier than Chinese.
  • This car is more expensive than mine.

🔸 Superlative (التفضيل بين مجموعة)

الصيغة: the + صفة قصيرة + est / the most + صفة طويلة

  • She is the youngest in the family.
  • That was the most difficult test.
  • He is the best doctor in the city.
