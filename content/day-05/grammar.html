📊 Comparative & Superlative (المقارنة والتفضيل)

🔹 Comparative Adjectives (المقارنة بين شيئين)

① الصفات القصيرة (+er + than)
shortshorter1
smallsmaller2
bigbigger3
talltaller4
② الصفات التي تنتهي بـ Y (Y → i + er)
easyeasier1
heavyheavier2
happyhappier3
busybusier4
③ الشواذ (Irregular)
goodbetter1
badworse2
farfarther3
④ الصفات الطويلة (More / Less + adj + than)
beautifulmore beautiful1
amazingmore amazing2
carefulmore careful3
interestingmore interesting4
stressfulmore stressful5
marvelousmore marvelous6

🔹 Superlative (التفضيل بين مجموعة)

الصفات القصيرة (the + adj + est)
tallthe tallest1
bigthe biggest2
easythe easiest3
goodthe best4
badthe worst5
الصفات الطويلة (the most / the least + adj)
beautifulthe most beautiful1
interestingthe most interesting2
carefulthe least careful3

🟰 as...as (المساواة)

Jimmy is as tall as his brother. (متساويان)
🚫 not as...as (نفي المساواة): Jimmy is not as fast as his father.
